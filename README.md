## bitrotlab

A small lab for file corruption, at **[bitrotlab.com](https://bitrotlab.com)**.

Files break quietly: a few flipped bits, a sector that no longer reads back right, a drive that dies on a Tuesday. bitrotlab shows what that actually looks like, with interactive demos that run in your browser:

- **[How parity works](https://bitrotlab.com/how-parity-works/)**: protect a picture with recovery data, damage it, and watch the lost pieces rebuilt, step by step.
- **[Hard drive failure calculator](https://bitrotlab.com/hard-drive-failure-calculator/)**: how likely you are to lose your files, from real drive failure rates and the copies you keep, RAID included.
- **[The most reliable hard drive?](https://bitrotlab.com/most-reliable-hard-drive/)**: what the failure data says, and why a second copy does more than a better drive.

bitrotlab is made by the makers of [FileGriffon](https://filegriffon.com). Writing: [bitrotlab on Substack](https://bitrotlab.substack.com).
